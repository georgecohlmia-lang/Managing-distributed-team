02Three foundations of distributed work

Distributed teams perform like a compromise when treated as one. Treated as deliberate design, they can outperform a single-location team. The foundations.

Written by default

Decisions, context, and status in writing. Verbal is supplement, not channel. Where will writing live? What tooling? What rhythm?

When synchronous earns its place

No status updates that could be written. Meetings exist for decisions and human signal — name the criteria.

Decision ownership across distance

Named ownership of every decision. Distributed teams break down when slow drift toward consensus becomes months of paralysis. Who owns what?

03Cadence translation — the three layers across distance

The cadence does not change because the team is distributed. If anything, it has to be enforced more strictly. How do the three layers from the management lesson translate to your configuration?

Weekly 1:1

Over video can be just as good — if the agenda is real, the camera is on, and you give it full attention.

All-hands across locations

Works on video with a deliberate ritual that travels — consistent opening, location spotlights, remote voices first in Q&A.

Monthly review

Benefits most from being in person. Quarterly in-person rhythm with monthly video reviews between, with each direct report.
